        Evidence, not proof

        People write like this too. That's where AI picked it up. AI detectors and human judges both get it wrong often. Treat each highlight as a question about your draft, not a verdict.

        Where the patterns come from

        Wikipedia's Signs of AI writing guide and Hunting the Muse's How to tell if writing is AI, with vocabulary lists dated by model era. Inspired by blader/humanizer.

        Fix it, not just find it

        The Fixed tab handles the mechanical part: filler, stiff words, em dashes, emoji bullets. For the rest, install it as a Claude skill and it rewrites your drafts properly. It can also learn your voice from a few samples. Setup is on GitHub.
