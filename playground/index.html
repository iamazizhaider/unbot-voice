        Questions

        How can I tell if writing is AI-generated?

        Look for clusters, not single words. The strongest signs are chatbot leftovers ("I hope this helps", "[Your Name]"), claims of importance in place of facts ("plays a pivotal role"), stock drama ("Here's the thing:"), words that spiked with each model era ("delve", "showcasing", "quietly"), "it's not X, it's Y", and heavy em dashes or emoji bullets. Paste your text above and each one is marked.

        What words does AI use too much?

        It depends on the model era. 2023–24 models overused "delve", "tapestry", "testament", "pivotal" and "intricate". 2024–25 models overused "showcasing", "fostering" and "enhance". In 2026 the tells are quieter: "quietly", "steady", "practical", "prioritize", "matters". One of these is fine. Several in one paragraph is the giveaway.

        How do I make AI writing sound more human?

        Replace general claims with specifics only you know: names, numbers, dates, what actually happened. Cut the setup phrases and say the point. Use plain verbs like "is" and "has". Drop the closing summary. This page applies the mechanical fixes for you. The facts have to come from you.

        Is unbot-voice an AI detector?

        No. It shows the habits that make writing read as AI so you can fix them. People write this way too, and AI detectors get it wrong often, so a high score is a reason to edit, not proof of anything.

        Is my text uploaded or stored?

        No. The scanner and the fixes run in your browser. Your draft is saved only in your own browser so a reload doesn't lose it.

        Why do em dashes make writing look like AI?

        Chat models used em dashes for asides far more often than most people do, so a draft full of them stands out. A comma, colon or full stop usually works better. Newer models were tuned to avoid them, so their absence proves nothing either.
