Sarah Chen is a visionary leader and passionate advocate for diversity in tech. With over 15 years of experience in software engineering, she has played a pivotal role in shaping the conversation around inclusive design. Her work serves as a testament to the power of combining technical excellence with empathy.

Throughout her career, Sarah has garnered widespread recognition for her innovative contributions, including being featured in Forbes, TechCrunch, and Wired. She currently serves as VP of Engineering at Lumen, where she leads a diverse team of 200 engineers, fostering a culture of collaboration, creativity, and continuous learning.

When she's not coding, Sarah enjoys hiking, reading, and spending time with her family.
