The AeroBrew Pro holds water at whatever temperature you set, to within one degree, from 88°C to 96°C. Light roasts taste better hotter and dark roasts taste better cooler, and most drip machines can't do either.

You set the temperature, the bloom time and the cup size on a dial on the front. It remembers three presets. The carafe is double-walled steel, so the coffee stays hot for about two hours without a hot plate cooking it.
