Most remote teams I've worked with don't have a productivity problem. They have a "nobody knows what anyone else is doing" problem, and people try to fix it with more meetings, which makes everyone tired.

This post covers what worked for the three teams I've managed since 2021: written weekly updates instead of standups, two hours of shared overlap a day, and a rule that a decision isn't made until it's written down somewhere searchable. None of it needs new software.
