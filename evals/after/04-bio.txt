Sarah Chen is VP of Engineering at Lumen, where she runs a team of about 200 engineers. Before that she spent nine years at Atlassian, where she led the accessibility overhaul of Jira's issue view and wrote the internal guide that became its public inclusive-design checklist.

She has been interviewed by Forbes and Wired about hiring practices in engineering. Outside work she hikes, mostly badly planned overnight trips in the Blue Mountains.
